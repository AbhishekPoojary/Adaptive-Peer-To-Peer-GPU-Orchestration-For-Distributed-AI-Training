Privacy

What this site keeps about you, who can see it, and how to remove it.

This site is run by one person for a group of friends or classmates. It trains image-recognition models on computers that members of the group lend. It has no ads, sells nothing, and shares nothing with anyone outside what is described here.

What is kept

  • Your account: your username, and either a scrambled (hashed) form of your password or, if you sign in with Google, your email address and Google's account ID. Nothing else from Google — not your contacts, files or profile.
  • What you upload and train: your picture datasets, your training jobs with their logs and progress charts, and your trained models.
  • In your browser: a sign-in token, kept only until you close the tab. No cookies, and no tracking or advertising of any kind.

Who can see it

  • You see everything of yours.
  • Other users see nothing of yours.
  • The person running the site can see that your jobs exist and whether they are running, but not your pictures, logs or models. They can delete a dataset to free up space.
  • The computer that trains your job receives your pictures while it trains and sends back the model. Those computers belong to members of the group, so only use this site within a group you trust.

Where it is stored

On the server that runs this site. It is not copied anywhere else.

Removing it

You can delete your datasets yourself on the Datasets page. To have your account and everything in it removed, ask the person who runs the site — whoever sent you the link.
